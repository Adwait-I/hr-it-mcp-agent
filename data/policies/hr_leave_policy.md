# HR Leave Policy

## Annual Leave Accrual

Employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited on January 1st.
- Accrual is prorated for employees who join mid-year (1.67 days per month worked).
- Maximum carryover at year-end is **5 days**, which must be used by the end of Q1 (March 31) of the following year; any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31.
- Annual leave may not be carried forward beyond Q1 without written approval from the HR Manager.

## Sick Leave Rules

Employees receive **8 days of paid sick leave** per calendar year.
- Sick leave does not carry over to the following year.
- For absences exceeding **2 consecutive days**, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning to work.
- Sick leave taken without a certificate (when required) may be reclassified as unpaid leave at the discretion of the HR Manager.

## Advance Notice Requirements

- For leave of **3 or more consecutive working days**, employees must submit a leave request at least **7 business days in advance** via the HR portal.
- Requests for fewer than 3 days may be submitted with 24 hours notice, subject to manager approval.
- Emergency leave (family bereavement, acute medical emergency) is exempt from advance notice requirements but must be documented upon return.

## Hybrid Work Policy

Employees on hybrid contracts are entitled to work remotely for up to **2 days per week**.
- Remote days must be agreed in advance with the direct line manager each week.
- Core in-office days are Tuesday and Thursday by default; exceptions require VP-level approval.
- Employees must remain reachable on corporate Slack and video conferencing tools during contracted hours while working remotely.
- Hybrid eligibility is reviewed annually during the performance review cycle.
